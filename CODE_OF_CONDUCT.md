# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be direct, respectful and constructive. Assume good intent, criticize the work rather
than the person, and keep discussion relevant to the repository.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, publishing someone else's private data, and deliberate
submission of credentials or secrets.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews in this repository.
Maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict participation. Report conduct
issues privately through a GitHub security advisory so the affected people are not
exposed publicly.
